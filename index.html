About this simulation & sources

Educational ascent and coast model using approximate historical thrust and mass profiles. Orbit targets are references; a full guidance, reentry, and landing model is not included.

Speed is Earth-centered inertial. The atmosphere rotates with Earth; a rocket on the pad has zero airspeed.
